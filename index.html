Flagged questions

While you practise, a collapsed bar lets you flag a question as Bad, Revisit, Useless or Important. Bad and Useless questions are left out at once; Important ones come back again and again.

Flagged ideas

On an idea you can open a collapsed bar and flag it as Bad, Revisit, Useless or Important. Bad and Useless ideas disappear from the idea lists at once (their questions stay). Important ideas are offered first in Keep me going. Idea flags are separate from question flags.
